**Q:** Tell me everything about the HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Growth.
**Answer (fact):** HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Growth is an Equity Mutual Fund Scheme launched by HDFC Mutual Fund on 10 Dec 1999, with a current AUM of ₹9,34,076 Cr and a Latest NAV of ₹1,164.95 as of 30 Oct 2026.
